Ian Hosten Coaching

All-In

The All-In coaching package.
This provides adynamically updated training schedule with continuous feedback.
Trainer is freely available.

Competition

The competition coaching package.
This provides a weekly updated training schedule with weekly feedback.
Trainer is not freely available during the week.

Basic

The basic coaching package.
This provides a monthly updated training schedule with only mothly feedback.
Trainer is not available during the month nor can the schedule be updated.
